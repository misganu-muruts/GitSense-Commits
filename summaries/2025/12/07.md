    *   **Phase 1 (First Page):** Involves an "optimistic fetch" with dynamic batch sizing and initial state caching.
    *   **Phase 2 (Subsequent Pages):** Relies on loading the cached state and managing "overflow transactions" for continuous paging.
3.  **Generic Programming:** A major architectural shift is the introduction of a generic pagination handler (`GenericPaginationHandler`) and a `TransactionProvider` interface.
