    *   Conditional truststore paths (`/tls-ca/ca.jks`, `/ca-trust-bundle/bundle.pem`) and commands for `keytool` operations based on whether AWS Private CA is trusted.
    *   Conditional `VAULT_CACERT` paths for `vaultify` init containers (`/ca-trust-bundle/bundle.pem` vs. `/vault_ca/ca.crt`).
    *   This suggests a transition or dual-support for certificate management, possibly moving towards AWS Private CA as a primary or alternative certificate issuer.

6.  **Resource Management:** Standardized resource requests and limits (`cpu`, `memory`) for JVM-based applications, with detailed JVM arguments (`-Xms`, `-Xmx`, `MaxMetaspaceSize`, `ReservedCodeCacheSize`, `UseG1GC`).

11. **Secrets Management:** Integration with Vault (`vaultify` init container) and AWS Secrets Manager (`secretman` init container) for fetching credentials and templating configuration files, ensuring secure handling of sensitive data.
